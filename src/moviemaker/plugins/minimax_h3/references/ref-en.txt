MiniMax H3 Ref2VA prompt guide
==============================

Reference-to-Video-and-Audio. Mention each reference by job:

Image 1 = identity / face (do not change age, ethnicity, or facial structure)
Image 2 = setting / background
Image 3 = wardrobe or product
Video 1 = motion reference
Audio 1 = voice or music bed

Syntax: refer to "Image 1", "Image 2" in the prompt. Keep one camera move per shot.
Timed shots still apply. Preserve identity across cuts.
