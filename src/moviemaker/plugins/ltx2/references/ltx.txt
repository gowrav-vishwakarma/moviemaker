LTX-2 prompt guide
==================

Write concise cinematic natural language. Include spoken lines in quotes.
Describe camera as part of the prose ("the camera slowly pans right...").
Optional camera_motion field: pan_right, dolly_in, static, handheld, orbit, crane_up.
Duration can be left to the model; when set, keep shots that fit the window.
Supports start/end keyframes and sliding-window continuation up to ~20s.
Distilled checkpoints use fewer steps (around 8). GGUF Q4 is the low-VRAM path.
