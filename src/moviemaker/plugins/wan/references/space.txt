Wan prompt guide (SPACE)
========================

Keep one shot per generation. Use SPACE:

Subject: who / what is on camera, appearance, wardrobe
Performance: action, expression, lip motion
Ambience: location, time of day, weather, practical lights
Camera: one move only (static, pan, tilt, dolly, tracking)
Extra: style, lens, film stock, negative constraints

Reference syntax: Image1, Video1, Audio1 (no spaces).
Prefer 80–150 words. Avoid multiple competing camera moves.
Wan 2.2 often uses two guidance phases (high/low noise).
